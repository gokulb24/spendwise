Privacy Policy — SpendWise

Last updated: 8 July 2026

SpendWise ("the app", "we") is a family expense-tracking app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided when you sign up with email/password or Google Sign-In (handled by Google Firebase Authentication).
  • Your financial data — the information you enter: expenses (amount, category, description, member, payment method, tags, notes), incomes, budgets, savings goals, loans/debts, investments, and reminders. Amounts are in Indian Rupees.
  • Family data — the family name, member names you add, and the invite code used to share a family across devices.
  • App settings — preferences such as theme and whether app-lock is enabled, stored only on your device.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no ads and we do not sell your data.

2. How your data is used

  • To provide the core service: recording expenses and syncing them live across the devices of your family members.
  • To generate your reports, charts, and summaries.
  • To send you the reminders you create (these fire locally on your device).

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and secured by access rules so that only signed-in members of your family can read or write your family's data. Data is encrypted in transit. Your settings and any locally-cached data remain on your device.

4. The optional AI Assistant

If you use the in-app AI Assistant, the questions you ask and a summary of your current month's tracker data (totals, categories, members, goals, debts, investments) are sent to Groq (groq.com), which runs the AI model, to generate a response. This happens only when you use that feature. See Groq's privacy policy for their handling of that data. You can avoid this by simply not using the AI Assistant.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) — accounts and data storage.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method.
  • Groq — only if you use the AI Assistant.

6. Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete individual entries in the app at any time. To delete your entire account and associated data, email us at the address below and we will remove it within 30 days.

7. Security

Access is protected by Firebase security rules and your login. You can also enable an in-app fingerprint/PIN lock. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
